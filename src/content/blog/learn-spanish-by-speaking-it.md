Most language apps teach you to recognise Spanish. You tap the right tile,
you match the word to the picture, and the streak goes up. Then someone
asks you a question in Spanish and nothing comes out.

The Spanish courses on Kuyun are built the other way round. Every lesson
is a scene — a morning in Mexico City, a market, a late bus — and the
lines in it are spoken aloud, in Latin American Spanish. You listen. Then
you hold the microphone and **say the line back**. Kuyun writes down what
it heard, and your study partner tells you how it sounded and what to try
again. Only then come the blanks, the quiz and the flash cards, checked on
the spot, with your answer sheet kept in the conversation so you can look
back at it.

## Six courses, one shelf

There are [six courses, from A1 to C2](https://kuyun.evoquake.com/c/spanish):

- **A1** — greeting people, introducing yourself, tú and usted.
- **A2** — the past tense, your day, a trip.
- **B1** — telling a story, holding your own in a conversation.
- **B2** — arguing a point, reading a short novel, following a film.
- **C1** — literature for pleasure, the subjunctive without effort.
- **C2** — any text, the Golden Age included.

Open one and the first lesson starts at once. Your study partner teaches a
step at a time, checks each answer and keeps your place, so you come back
to where you were, not to the beginning.

When you are ready to read, there is the library
[*Spanish, from A1 to C2*](https://kuyun.evoquake.com/p/p_a50cff0b4b): the
six courses and a growing shelf of Spanish books read aloud, each marked
with its level and why — from graded readers up to Cervantes — and each
with a reading companion that answers any line you ask about, without
spoiling what comes next.

## What it costs

Nothing, every day. Every Kuyun account has a free allowance that comes
back each morning, and a lesson fits inside it comfortably. There is no
streak to protect and no paywall between A1 and C2.

## Why it works

Speaking is the part of a language you cannot learn by tapping, and it is
the part most apps leave out because it is hard to judge. Kuyun listens,
writes what it heard, and has a partner say how it went — the same loop a
good tutor runs, lesson after lesson, for anyone who opens the course.
